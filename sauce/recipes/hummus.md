# Hummus

Some people say they are morally against this delicious dish, but I do not understand why.

*vegan, dip*

**4 servings**

---

- *1/4 cup* tahini
- *1/4 cup* lemon juice
- *1 can* garbanzo beans
- *2 heaping tablespoons* jarlic
- *4 pinches* smoked paprika
- *4 pinches* cayenne pepper
- *2 tablespoons* olive oil
- *3-4 tablespoons* tap water


---

1. In a small bowl, mix together the tahini and the lemon juice, until its creamy.
2. Add the garlic, paprika, and cayenne pepper, and combine uniformly into the mixture.
3. Add the olive oil to the food processor.
4. Add the bowl contents to the food processor, and blend until smooth.
5. Drain the can of garbanzo beans, and add half to the food processor, and blend until smooth.
6. Add the second half of the garbanzo beans to the food processor and blend until smooth.
7. Add 3-4 tablespoons of tap water to the food processor and blend until the desired consistency.
